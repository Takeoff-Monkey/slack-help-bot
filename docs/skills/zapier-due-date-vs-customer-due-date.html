What it does

When the customer's due date is set/changed on a Monday item, this Zap runs a due-date check to make sure the internal "due date" is earlier than the customer's expected delivery. If the internal date is on or after the customer's date, the US team is alerted so expectations can be realigned before the customer is impacted.

How to trigger / use

  • Triggered automatically when the customer due date column is set/changed on a Monday item
  • Internal due-date logic runs to compare the two dates

Verify success

  • Due-date check field on the item updates
  • Confirmation email logic aligns with the requested vs internal due date
  • If misaligned, the US team gets an alert

Common failure modes

  • Status labels changed and the Zap's filter no longer matches
  • Date columns renamed on Monday
  • Timezone edge cases (e.g., customer in PT, item logged in CT) cause borderline misfires
  • Customer edits the date after "sent to TOM" and the change doesn't propagate

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link
  • Requested due date (customer's)
  • Confirmation email due date (internal)
  • Screenshots of both date columns
