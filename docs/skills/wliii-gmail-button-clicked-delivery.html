What it does

Configured on Work Log III: when the configured button column on an item is clicked, an email is sent to ALL Recipients (the Delivery recipients list) and to the address(es) in the item's Email Address column.

Common failure modes

  • Permissions on Gmail integration.
  • Renamed columns (Button, ALL Recipients (Delivery), Email Address).
  • Missing / malformed email in the Email Address column or empty Recipients list.

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link + item ID.
  • Timestamp of the button click.
  • Screenshots of the Button, Recipients, and Email Address columns.
