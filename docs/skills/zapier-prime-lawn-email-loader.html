What it does

One of our family of customer email-to-Monday loaders. Bid emails sent to the customer-aliased address prime@takeoffmonkey.com (which forwards to bids@..) are parsed and turned into items on Prime Lawn's bid board with sender, subject, and any attachments captured.

How to trigger / use

  • Send (or have the customer send) a bid email to prime@takeoffmonkey.com
  • The Zap matches on the alias and writes an item to the Prime Lawn bid board

Verify success

  • A new item appears on Prime Lawn's bid board within a minute or two of the email arriving
  • Subject, sender, and attachments are populated

Common failure modes

  • Email forwarded from a non-standard address breaks the parser
  • Attachment too large or in an unexpected format
  • Gmail filter / alias misrouted
  • Monday board/group mapping changed

What to collect before escalating

  • Original email subject + sender + timestamp
  • Whether attachments were included
  • Expected board (Prime Lawn bid board) and item ID if partially created
