What it does

Demo variant of our family of customer email-to-Monday loaders. Bid emails sent to the demo address yourcompany@takeoffmonkey.com (which forwards to bids@..) are parsed and turned into items on the demo bid board so we can show prospects the workflow end-to-end.

How to trigger / use

  • Send a demo bid email to yourcompany@takeoffmonkey.com
  • The Zap matches on the alias and writes an item to the demo bid board

Verify success

  • A new item appears on the demo bid board within a minute or two
  • Subject, sender, and attachments are populated

Common failure modes

  • Demo Zap turned off after a sales/demo cycle and not re-enabled
  • Email forwarded from a non-standard address breaks the parser
  • Monday board/group mapping changed

What to collect before escalating

  • Original email subject + sender + timestamp
  • Whether the Zap is currently turned on in Zapier
  • Expected demo board name
