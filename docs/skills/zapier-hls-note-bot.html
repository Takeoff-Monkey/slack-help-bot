What it does

Listens for note emails generated from the giddyup client platform (HLS) and posts the note content as an update on the corresponding Monday item, so the team sees client notes directly on the job.

How to trigger / use

  • Triggered automatically when an HLS/giddyup notification email arrives
  • The Zap matches the note to a Monday item and posts the body as an update

Verify success

  • A Monday update appears on the relevant HLS item with the note content
  • The matched item corresponds to the job referenced in the giddyup email

Common failure modes

  • Item match fails (job identifier in email doesn't match a Monday item)
  • Gmail filter / inbox routing changed and the trigger doesn't fire
  • Note body is empty or HTML-only and parsing fails

What to collect before escalating

  • Original giddyup email (subject + sender + timestamp)
  • Expected Monday item link / job identifier
  • Screenshot of the item's Updates section
