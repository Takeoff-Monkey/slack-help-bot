What it does

When someone submits the contact form on the takeoffmonkey.com website, the form generates an email. This Zap watches for those emails, parses out the sender's name/contact info and the message, and creates a Monday item on the leads/contact board so the sales team can follow up.

How to trigger / use

  • Triggered automatically by the contact-form email landing in Gmail
  • No manual trigger required

Verify success

  • A new item appears on the leads/contact Monday board for the submission
  • Name, email, phone, and message body are populated

Common failure modes

  • Website form template changed and the parser no longer extracts fields cleanly
  • Form emails being routed to spam
  • Monday board column renamed

What to collect before escalating

  • Submission timestamp + sender name/email if known
  • The form-generated email itself (subject + body)
  • Expected Monday board / item ID if partially created
