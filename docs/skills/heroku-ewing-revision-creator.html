What it does

Listens in Slack for the trigger phrase #revise! followed by a job number in the form #12345!. When triggered, it:

  • Locates the existing completed job on Monday
  • Creates a new Monday item as a numbered revision of that job
  • Recycles the original Google Doc instructions so they're tied to the new revision item

How to trigger / use

  • In a Slack channel the bot is invited to, post a message containing #revise! and the job number #12345!

Verify success

  • A new revision item appears on the appropriate Monday board, numbered as a revision of the original
  • The Google Doc instructions are reused / linked to the new revision item

What to collect before escalating

  • Slack channel + permalink to the message that triggered (or failed to trigger) the bot
  • Original job number and the expected revision number
  • Link to the original Monday item / Google Doc if known
  • Screenshot of any error or missing-response behavior
