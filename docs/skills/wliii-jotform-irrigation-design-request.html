What it does

Configured on Work Log III: when a Status column changes to a specified value, the Jotform "Irrigation Design Request Form" is assigned to the address(es) in the item's Email Address column (i.e., a pre-filled form link is sent for the recipient to complete).

Common failure modes

  • Permissions on Jotform integration.
  • Renamed columns or status labels (Status, Email Address).
  • Missing / malformed email in the Email Address column.
  • Jotform form deleted, renamed, or unpublished.

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link + item ID.
  • Timestamp of the status change.
  • Screenshots of Status and Email Address columns.
