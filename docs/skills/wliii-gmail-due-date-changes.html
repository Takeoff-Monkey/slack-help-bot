What it does

Configured on Work Log III: when an item's Due Date column changes, the automation fires unless Status is Pending. It then emails the address(es) in the item's Email Address column to notify of the new due date.

Common failure modes

  • Permissions on Gmail integration.
  • Renamed columns or status labels (Status, Due Date, Email Address).
  • Missing / malformed email in the Email Address column.
  • Status was Pending at trigger time (by design, no email is sent).

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link + item ID.
  • Timestamp of the due date change.
  • Screenshots of Due Date, Status, and Email Address columns.
