What it does

Configured on Work Log III: when an item's Status column transitions from Pending to Uploaded, an email is sent to the address(es) in the item's Email Address column.

Common failure modes

  • Permissions on Gmail integration.
  • Renamed columns or status labels (Status, Email Address).
  • Missing / malformed email in the Email Address column.

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link + item ID.
  • Timestamp of the status change.
  • Screenshots of Status and Email Address columns.
