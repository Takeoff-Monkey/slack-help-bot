What it does

Configured on Work Log III: when an item's Status transitions to Prep in Process, the automation posts a notification to the #general Slack channel and starts the Prep time-tracking timer on the item.

Common failure modes

  • Permissions on Slack integration (workspace re-auth required).
  • Renamed Status label or renamed Slack channel.
  • Time tracking column renamed or removed.

What to collect before escalating

  • Item link + item ID.
  • Timestamp of the status change.
  • Screenshots of Status column and the Prep time-tracking column.
  • Whether a Slack message appeared in #general.
